# Bahja — Final Visual Direction v1

## Mission
Elevate the existing Arabic handmade storefront into an authored commerce experience without changing product facts, prices, availability, routes, or order mechanics.

## North star
**Quiet editorial structure × joyful handmade identity × tactile product storytelling × effortless Arabic commerce.**

Reference roles:
- TOTEME — structure, whitespace, restraint, product hierarchy.
- Cult Gaia — product theatre and signature editorial moments.
- Sézane — warmth, storytelling, collection merchandising.
- Mlouye — object-led product presentation and personalization.
- Level Shoes — Arabic/RTL commerce clarity.
- Ounass — shop/PDP information architecture.

No reference is copied literally.

## Brand feel
Warm, handmade, feminine without cliché, Egyptian without folkloric decoration, personal, craft-led, refined, intimate.

## Shape language
Reduce rounded cards, borders, shadows and pill UI. Use whitespace, image scale and typography to separate chapters. Rounded controls remain acceptable where they improve interaction.

## Image policy
Evidence before decoration. Every large image must provide product, material, scale, color, construction or lifestyle proof. Do not use generated imagery as final product evidence.

## Typography
Arabic is primary. Display typography should carry warmth and personality; body/UI typography should remain highly legible. English is reserved for real product/collection names and compact metadata, not duplicated in every navigation label.

## Motion
One restrained language: subtle image reveal, tiny product-image hover scale, short editorial transitions. No heavy parallax, WebGL, autoplay carousels or animation on every element.

## Commerce
Home sells the world. Shop sells the catalogue. PDP proves the piece. Cart closes the decision. Do not propagate Home's editorial treatment into Shop/PDP/Cart until the Home gate passes.

## Personalization
Customization is a core product value. Present only verified options: size, color, thread and chain/detail choices where they are actually available, then hand off to WhatsApp.

## Home gate
The first five seconds must feel like a distinct Bahja brand, not merely a cleaner boutique template. If the new Home is only different rather than clearly stronger, revise before propagating.
