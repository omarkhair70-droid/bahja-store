# Bahja — Home Gate Brief v1

Scope: **Home only.** Shop, product detail and cart remain visually untouched until this gate passes.

## 01 Header
Home-specific restrained RTL header. Strong Bahja identity, concise Arabic navigation, cart visibility, compact mobile menu. No duplicated Arabic/English nav pills.

## 02 Hero
Large editorial image. Primary line: **للغُرز حكايا… وهُنا لكل غرزة حكاية**. One primary commerce CTA and one quiet custom-order CTA. No floating panel, trust-chip cluster or decorative card shell.

## 03 Featured pieces
Asymmetric product composition: one lead piece + supporting pieces. Product information lives beneath/alongside imagery without card chrome.

## 04 Elegant Clutch
Signature product chapter using the existing real Clutch gallery. Treat the piece as an object: large imagery plus detail/held/interior proof. Link to the real PDP.

## 05 Collection worlds
Himalayan Thread and Chain Thread as two large visual chapters, not two rounded cards.

## 06 Craft/material proof
Use existing real texture/hardware/detail crops. Present as material evidence only; do not fabricate maker/process imagery.

## 07 Make it yours
Editorial personalization strip for verified choices: size, color, thread and chain/details, ending in the existing custom-order/WhatsApp path.

## 08 Small joys
Hair accessories and canvas art get deliberately lighter visual weight so bags stay dominant.

## 09 Bahja story
Use only verified brand language and existing product/craft evidence. Do not invent a founder story or production claims.

## 10 Final CTA
Quiet closing conversion: shop + WhatsApp/custom order. No large rounded promotional panel.

## Mobile
Compose independently: vertical hero, full-width collection chapters, clear touch targets, concise copy, no horizontal overflow.

## Gate QA
- npm ci
- lint
- production build
- desktop/mobile screenshots
- no broken images
- no horizontal overflow
- menu/cart route smoke
- reduced-motion compatibility
- manual comparison against the reference principles

Stop if the page looks merely cleaner rather than authored.
